Barra China Equity Model (CNE5) - Complete Descriptor Definitions
==================================================================

STYLE FACTOR 1: Beta
--------------------
Definition: 1.00 · BETA

BETA (β):
- Time-series regression of excess stock return (r_t - rf_t) against cap-weighted excess return of universe R_t
- Formula: r_t - rf_t = α + β·R_t + e_t
- Lookback: 252 trading days
- Half-life: 63 trading days


STYLE FACTOR 2: Momentum
------------------------
Definition: 1.00 · RSTR

RSTR (Relative Strength):
- Sum of excess log returns over trailing T=504 trading days with lag L=21 days
- Formula: RSTR = Σ(t=L to T+L) w_t · [ln(1+r_t) - ln(1+rf_t)]
- Exponential weight half-life: 126 trading days


STYLE FACTOR 3: Size
--------------------
Definition: 1.00 · LNCAP

LNCAP (Natural log of market cap):
- ln(total market capitalization)


STYLE FACTOR 4: Earnings Yield
-------------------------------
Definition: 0.68 · EPIBS + 0.11 · ETOP + 0.21 · CETOP

EPIBS (Analyst Predicted Earnings-to-Price):
- Earnings-to-price ratio forecasted by analysts

ETOP (Trailing Earnings-to-Price):
- Trailing 12-month earnings / current market cap
- Trailing earnings = last fiscal year earnings + (current interim - previous year interim)

CETOP (Cash Earnings-to-Price):
- Cash earnings / market cap
- Cash earnings = earnings + depreciation + amortization


STYLE FACTOR 5: Residual Volatility
------------------------------------
Definition: 0.74 · DASTD + 0.16 · CMRA + 0.10 · HSIGMA

DASTD (Daily Standard Deviation):
- Standard deviation of daily returns over trailing 252 trading days
- Exponential weight half-life: 42 trading days

CMRA (Cumulative Range):
- ln(1 + max cumulative return) - ln(1 + min cumulative return)
- Measured over trailing 12 months

HSIGMA (Historical Sigma):
- Volatility of residual returns from factor model
- Computed from trailing 252 trading days
- Exponential weight half-life: 63 trading days


STYLE FACTOR 6: Growth
-----------------------
Definition: 0.18 · SGRO + 0.24 · EGRO + 0.47 · EGIBS + 0.11 · EGIBS_s

SGRO (Sales Growth):
- 5-year regression slope of log sales against time
- Requires at least 3 years of data

EGRO (Earnings Growth):
- 5-year regression slope of log earnings against time
- Requires at least 3 years of data

EGIBS (Predicted Earnings Growth):
- Analyst predicted long-term earnings growth rate

EGIBS_s (Short-term Predicted Earnings Growth):
- (FY2 predicted EPS - FY1 predicted EPS) / |FY1 predicted EPS|


STYLE FACTOR 7: Book-to-Price
------------------------------
Definition: 1.00 · BTOP

BTOP (Book-to-Price):
- Book value of common equity / market cap


STYLE FACTOR 8: Leverage
-------------------------
Definition: 0.38 · MLEV + 0.35 · DTOA + 0.27 · BLEV

MLEV (Market Leverage):
- (Market equity + preferred equity + long-term debt + short-term debt) / market equity

DTOA (Debt-to-Assets):
- Total debt / total assets

BLEV (Book Leverage):
- (Book equity + preferred equity + long-term debt + short-term debt) / book equity


STYLE FACTOR 9: Liquidity
--------------------------
Definition: 0.35 · STOM + 0.35 · STOQ + 0.30 · STOA

STOM (Share Turnover 1 Month):
- ln(monthly trading volume / shares outstanding)
- Trailing 1 month

STOQ (Share Turnover 1 Quarter):
- ln(quarterly trading volume / shares outstanding)
- Trailing 3 months

STOA (Share Turnover 1 Year):
- ln(annual trading volume / shares outstanding)
- Trailing 12 months


STYLE FACTOR 10: Non-linear Size
---------------------------------
Definition: 1.00 · NLSIZE

NLSIZE (Non-linear Size):
- Cube of size factor after orthogonalization
- NLSIZE = (LNCAP)³ after removing correlation with LNCAP


DATA REQUIREMENTS SUMMARY
=========================

Price Data (Daily):
- Stock returns (r_t)
- Risk-free rate (rf_t)
- Market cap-weighted index returns (R_t)
- Trading volume
- Shares outstanding

Fundamental Data (Quarterly/Annual):
- Total market capitalization
- Book value of common equity
- Earnings (trailing 12-month, fiscal year, interim)
- Sales (5-year history)
- Cash flow (depreciation, amortization)
- Total debt (long-term + short-term)
- Total assets
- Preferred equity

Analyst Data:
- Predicted EPS (FY1, FY2)
- Long-term earnings growth forecasts
- Earnings-to-price forecasts

Lookback Periods:
- 21 days (momentum lag)
- 42 days (DASTD half-life)
- 63 days (beta half-life, HSIGMA half-life)
- 126 days (RSTR weight half-life)
- 252 days (1 year daily data)
- 504 days (2 years for momentum)
- 5 years (growth factors)
